# Shoulder Surfing Reloaded x Valkyrien Skies Compat

Makes Shoulder Surfing Reloaded actually work when you're riding a mount on a Valkyrien Skies / Eureka ship. Forge 1.20.1.

SSR officially marks VS as incompatible. The two fight over the camera and you end up with weird angles, the camera not following your mouse, and the player rendering centered instead of off-shoulder. This patches over all of that.

## What it does

- Keeps your shoulder offset on a ship, even when the ship rolls.
- Camera follows your mouse on a ship.
- Stops the shoulder shift from disappearing when you pitch the camera down.
- Hides SSR's incompatibility warning for VS, since this mod is the reason both can coexist.

Off-ship behavior is untouched.

## Requires

Minecraft 1.20.1, Forge 47+, SSR 4.22.0+, Valkyrien Skies 2 2.3.0+. Works with [Eureka](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/eureka-ships-for-valkyrien-skies) too.
